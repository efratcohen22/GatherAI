# User guide

1. Sign in, or use the fictional demo account shown on the local login page.
2. Open **Monsey Community Shabbat Weekend**. The overview shows live counts and **Needs your attention**.
3. Open **AI Assistant** and click "One of our drivers just cancelled." Review the proposal, including who could not be placed.
4. Approve it. Rides update and notification drafts appear under **Messages**.
5. Open **Chat simulator** and try registering, requesting a ride, or cancelling.
6. In **Event settings**, copy the public registration link.

Sending a message in this version reaches only the simulator.
